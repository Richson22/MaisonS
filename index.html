About Maison S

A House for Good Things to Happen

Maison S is a private culinary and creative atelier in Abuja.

A home where chefs, creators, communities and brands come together through thoughtfully considered experiences.

Maison S is not a restaurant.

It is a place for the table to become whatever the occasion calls for — a private dinner, a brunch, a cocktail afternoon, a creative gathering, a collaboration or simply a beautiful afternoon with your people.

At the heart of Maison S is the belief that hospitality is not only about what is served, but about how a place makes people feel.
